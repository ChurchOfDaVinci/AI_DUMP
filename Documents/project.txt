Project Summary
Develop a 400A AC/DC charger (battery charger) option for series production in the e-PU10 BESS

Goal / Objective
A 400A (one power lock set), AC/DC charger battery charger with galvanic isolation between AC/DC to be used for charging the main battery (600-800VDC) of the e-PU10.

It is required that the system is highly reliable > 98% uptime, is easily serviceable (ideally without interruption of the machine) and is liquid cooled. Furthermore a galvanic isolation between AC/DC is mandatory to overcome earthing conflicts between AC supply and microgrid.

The installation should be compliant with IEC 61439, NEN 1010 and whatever is applicable.
The full product must be ready before November 2026.

Context / Background
Based on client request a 400A input charger is asked. The following specification are must have:

Galvanic isolation between AC / DC
400/500A Powerlock set (3P + PE)
Unidirectional
Liquid cooled
DC voltage range 600 - 800 VDC at least. (wider is fine)
material cost < 20000 euro.
Industrial control/monitoring protocol: ModbusTCP (favourable), CAN, profinet or Ethercat.
EU compliant
Mobile applicable / Vibration resistance.
Minimum power setteable at least 55 kW / 80A.
Accepteable inrush behaviour for AC source (C-type circuit breaker) (C80)
The following specification are recommended:

Multi module setup in power steps of minimal 30kw and maximal 100 kW.
Serviceable without BESS microgrid interruption
High reliability > 98% per system
PFC power supply.
Minimum power setteable at least 20 kW / 32A.
Accepteable inrush behaviour for AC source (B-type circuit breaker) (B32)
e-PU10 Restrications / considerations

Size should not exceed powermodule size.
Industrial EMC or better (IEC - 61000)
Total system heat production < 7kW --> Efficiency higher than 97.5%
System weight less than 500 kg.
DC Leakeage current is lower than e-PU10 battery precharge power capacity.
